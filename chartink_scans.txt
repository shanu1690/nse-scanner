NSE SCANNER -> CHARTINK / SCREENER.IN PORT
===========================================
These replicate our FADE/DELIVERY scanner as platform scans so you can
view results from anywhere (phone/app). Copy-paste into Chartink Scanner
or Screener.in. They match the SIGNALS, not bit-for-bit picks (platforms
can't compute the weighted composite score, VOL-z, or relative strength
vs NIFTY inside a scan).

------------------------------------------------------------
1) CHARTINK - FADE (beaten-down names expecting a bounce)
   This is the default style in our config.yaml.
------------------------------------------------------------
Go to chartink.com/screener -> New scan -> replace formula with:

close < ema(close, 50)
and rsi(14) < 45
and roc(close, 20) < 0
and close > highest(high, 250) * 0.80
and volume > avg(volume, 20)

Optional extras (same as our sub-scores):
  - 23% beaten down:   and close < highest(high, 250) * 0.90
  - Extra oversold:    and rsi(14) < 40
  - Rising volume:     and volume > avg(volume, 20) * 1.2
  - Above 200 dma:     and close > ema(close, 200)

How to read results: sort by "close to 52w low %" - lowest = most
washed out. These are the FADE-BUY candidates. Stops/targets are NOT
on Chartink - use ~1.5x ATR for your stop (atr(14) column) - or keep
using our web UI for exact entry/stop/T1/T2.

------------------------------------------------------------
2) CHARTINK - MOMENTUM (strong names continuing up)
   Run if you switch config to style: momentum
------------------------------------------------------------
close > ema(close, 50)
and close > ema(close, 200)
and rsi(14) > 55
and roc(close, 20) > 0
and close > highest(high, 250) * 0.90
and volume > avg(volume, 20)

------------------------------------------------------------
3) SCREENER.IN - FADE (EOD data, good for the same idea)
------------------------------------------------------------
At screener.in -> Screener -> add filters:

  RSI 14 (Value)      < 45
  Current Price       below  50 Day MA
  Return % (3 weeks)  negative            (pick "Low to High")
  Current Price       > 80% of 52 Week High   (field: "52 Week High")
  % Deliverable Qty   > 60                  (delivery strength filter)

Sort by "Near 52 Week Low %" ascending. Same idea as our fade list.
(Note: screener.in updates after market close, not intraday.)

------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT CANNOT BE REPLICATED
------------------------------------------------------------
- Relative strength vs NIFTY (our only factor with a real edge).
- The 0-100 composite score and exact top-20 ordering.
- Entry / Stop / T1 / T2 levels (ATR-based) - these platforms don't
  compute trade levels.
- The OPTIONS picks (CE strike, premium, breakeven, PCR, max pain) -
  not available as a screener; Chartink's option tools are OI-based only.
- Your live SCORECARD / journal (who won, who lost) - unique to us.

=> Best setup: use Chartink/Screener for the daily shortlist from your
   phone, then confirm entry/stop/targets + the scorecard in our web UI
   (or the 8:45am email).
